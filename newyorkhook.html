New York Hook

The New York hook has many uses such as

  1. Pulling ceiling
  2. Pulling wood floors
  3. Forcable entry
  4. breaching walls

The New York Hook can be used with the Halligan Bar to breach doors It can also be used to pull ceilings and wood floors to search for extension of the fire. Here is a video of the ways a New York hook can be used
